How Local Clip Studio Works

Select a supported gameplay recording, video file, or URL.

02 — Create and customize

Generate highlights or commentary-based videos with audio, captions, and platform-appropriate formatting.

03 — Review the output

Local Clip Studio currently prepares TikTok-ready videos and matching captions for manual uploading. Additional authorized TikTok publishing functionality is under development and subject to TikTok's API requirements, permissions, and review process.

About Local Clip Studio

Local Clip Studio is a desktop-based video production application designed to simplify repetitive editing and content preparation. It combines media processing, creative tools, and publishing workflows in one interface.

External platform connections are optional and require authorization by the account holder. Platform functionality is subject to the availability and policies of each service.
