Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Local Clip Studio is a desktop application for creating, editing, organizing, and preparing videos for publication on social media platforms.

This Privacy Policy explains the information processed through our website and desktop application, including optional connections to third-party social platforms.

1. Information We Process

When using Local Clip Studio, the application may process:

  • Video recordings, gameplay footage, audio commentary, and related media files selected by the user.
  • Video titles, descriptions, captions, hashtags, thumbnails, and publishing schedules.
  • Application settings and local processing logs.
  • Social media account identifiers and authorization credentials required for enabled integrations.
  • Publishing status, upload results, and related information returned by connected platforms.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is processed to provide requested application functions, including:

  • Creating and editing videos.
  • Combining commentary audio with gameplay footage.
  • Generating captions and preparing publication metadata.
  • Organizing video files and publishing queues.
  • Uploading or scheduling content on connected platforms when authorized by the account holder.
  • Reporting errors and supporting application operation.

3. Local Storage

Local Clip Studio is designed primarily to operate on the user's computer. Videos, application settings, publishing records, and authentication information may be stored in local application folders.

Users are responsible for securing access to their computer and locally stored files. Some application functions may contact external services to perform authorized operations.

4. Third-Party Platform Integrations

Local Clip Studio supports or is developing optional integrations with services such as YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok.

When an account holder authorizes an integration, the application may access the information and permissions necessary to perform requested publishing functions.

Videos, captions, and related information may be transmitted to the selected platform when a user enables uploading or publishing. Each external platform processes information under its own privacy policy and terms.

TikTok automatic publishing functionality is under development and is subject to TikTok authorization, API permissions, and applicable review requirements.

5. Authorization and Account Access

Connected-platform permissions are requested through the applicable provider's authorization process. The application uses the permissions granted by the account holder to perform authorized functions.

Users may revoke access through the relevant social media platform's connected-app or security settings. Revoking access may prevent future publishing operations until the account is reconnected.

6. Website Information

The Local Clip Studio informational website does not intentionally include advertising trackers or custom visitor analytics.

The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information associated with visits, such as connection information, in accordance with its own policies.

If you contact us by email, we may use the information you provide to respond to your inquiry.

7. Data Sharing

We do not sell users' personal information.

Information may be transmitted to third-party platforms or technical service providers when necessary to perform functions requested or authorized by the user.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored information generally remains on the user's computer until removed or replaced by the user or through application operations.

Users may remove locally stored media and application data and revoke connected-platform permissions. Removing local data does not necessarily remove content already uploaded to an external social media service.

Requests concerning personal information under our control may be directed to the contact email below. Information held by external platforms is subject to their separate data controls and policies.

9. Information Security

We aim to handle application information responsibly and limit access to the permissions necessary for requested features.

No method of digital storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure. Users should protect their computers, credentials, and connected accounts.

10. Children's Privacy

Local Clip Studio is not designed for children under the minimum age required by the applicable services. Users must comply with the age requirements of the platforms they connect.

11. Your Choices

Users can choose which media files to process, which platform integrations to authorize, and whether to use available publishing functions.

For privacy questions, requests concerning information under our control, or assistance with account disconnection, please contact us.

12. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application features, integrations, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be made available on this page with an updated revision date.
